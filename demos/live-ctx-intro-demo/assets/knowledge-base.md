# Acme Internal Knowledge Base

## Expense policy
Employees may expense meals up to $60/day while traveling. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
Receipts are required for any item above $25. Submit within 30 days via the Finance portal.

## Laptop refresh
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Engineers may request 32GB RAM models.
Open a ticket with IT; approval takes 2 business days.

## On-call rotation
Backend on-call rotates weekly, Monday 9am UTC. The primary is paged first, the secondary after 10 minutes.
Escalation goes to the team lead after 30 minutes without acknowledgement.

## Deployment freeze
No production deploys from Dec 20 to Jan 3. Hotfixes need VP approval during the freeze.

## Vacation policy
25 days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over. Requests need manager approval 2 weeks ahead.

## Office Wi-Fi
Network: acme-corp. Guests use acme-guest, password rotates monthly and is posted at reception.

## Code review rules
Every PR needs one approval. PRs touching `payments/` need two, one from the payments team.
CI must be green before merge. Squash merges only.
